4-STAGE AXIAL COMPRESSOR  (pi = 1.12, 20,000 rpm) - CAD MODEL, reference layout
==========================================================================================
Units mm. Shaft axis = global Z, flow towards +Z. Every part is modelled in assembly
coordinates, so all parts drop into place when inserted at the origin.
Part names, shapes and stacking follow the reference 4-stage assembly you supplied
(module pitch 32 mm; stage stack 4 x 32 = 128 mm).

FILES
  Compressor_4stage_assembly.step   whole assembly (product tree with 40 parts, colours as reference)
  parts_STEP/*.step              one STEP file per part
  parts_STL/*.stl                mesh of every part (for the slicer)
  Pictures/                      renders of the assembly, rotor 1, stator-casing 1, front frame, shaft
  BuildCompressor_SolidWorks.bas macro: STEP parts -> SLDPRT (bodies combined) -> SLDASM  (untested)

OPEN IN SOLIDWORKS (two ways)
  A) File > Open > Compressor_4stage_assembly.step. SolidWorks builds an assembly and one part per
     component; File > Save As keeps them as native SLDASM/SLDPRT.
  B) Run the macro (Tools > Macro > Run) and choose this folder.
  Parts with blades (R*_rotor, S*_stator_casing) and the frames/spools/shaft are multi-body: the hub or
  casing body and each blade are separate closed solids that overlap by 0.6 mm. Merge them with
  Insert > Features > Combine (Add) - the macro does this automatically - to get one solid per part
  like the reference parts.

KEY DIMENSIONS (from the design calculations PDF)
  Rotor tip  Ø100 (tip corners on R50.0) / casing, frame and spool bore Ø101 -> 0.5 mm radial (1 mm diametral)
  Stator vanes end 0.5 mm radially above the rotating hub (1 mm diametral)
  Hub line   Ø75.00 at inlet -> Ø76.88 at outlet (constant tip, constant Ca)
  Rotor blades per stage: 38, 40, 41, 19      Stator vanes per stage: 27, 39, 42, 26
  Rotor: boss Ø31 with Ø25.2 bore and flat at x = 11.6 (D-bore), web 3 mm at z = 5..8, rim 3 mm
  Shaft: Ø25, flat at x = 11.4 over z = 0..128, Ø20 threaded ends z = -30..-20 and 148..178
  Bearings 7005 (25x47x12) in front/rear bearing frames, caps with 3 x M3, M20 lock nuts, spacers Ø32/Ø25.2 x 8
  Tie rods 4 x M4 on R60 at 45/135/225/315 deg, z = -76..206; base plate 150 x 282 x 8 mm

PRINTING NOTES (PLA Basic)
  Rotors: axis vertical, 4-5 perimeters, 100 % infill near the boss, fine layers (0.12 mm) for the blades.
  Ream the rotor D-bore to a sliding fit on the shaft; balance each rotor.
  Stator-casings, frames and spools: axis vertical, 30-40 % infill. Drill the Ø2 static-pressure tap
  holes through the spool wall after printing (bosses are modelled). Base plate is 282 mm long:
  print in two halves or cut it from aluminium / acrylic sheet.
