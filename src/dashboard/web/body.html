Related work

Y. Su and X. Chen, “Real-time simulation and abnormal condition recognition of series compressor system based on digital twin,” Digital Engineering, vol. 7, 100059, 2025. doi:10.1016/j.dte.2025.100059. Open the paper

Like this project, they build a compressor twin on a physics model (lumped mass, momentum and energy conservation) and set its boundary conditions from sensors at 8 Hz. They validate it on a real rig (1.88 % and 0.67 % pressure error, 3.72 % flow error) and detect surge from pressure and flow signals. This twin adds the course methods they do not use: POD of the stage field, a Kalman filter that keeps health factors in sync with the rig, CUSUM drift detection and a return path to the motor.
